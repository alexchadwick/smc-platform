About us

The story of Steve’s Mediterranean Chef began many years ago in Lebanon with the Chammas family gathering together every Sunday to enjoy traditional Mediterranean dishes perfected and passed down through generations

Today, Steve’s Mediterranean brings generations old, authentic Lebanese recipes to local grocery stores. At Steve’s, we have a special connection to our food and how it’s made and are committed to delivering real food made by real people. We put our heart and soul into every product, and you can taste the difference.
